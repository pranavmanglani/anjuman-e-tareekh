Culinary Glossary

Kitchen Terminology

Bhunao A slow process of frying and reducing spices.
Deg A large traditional copper or iron cooking vessel.
Dum Slow cooking sealed under pressure using dough.
Kewra A fragrant floral distillate used in desserts and rice.
Zafrani A preparation or dish flavoured with saffron.
Tarka Aromatic tempering of spices added to a dish.
