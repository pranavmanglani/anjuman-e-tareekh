Janmashtami Celebrations

Tradition on a Plate: Celebrating Janmashtami and the Culinary Heritage of Lucknow

As part of the Janmashtami celebrations, Anjuman-e-Tareekh organised a beautiful culinary event that brought together the festive spirit of Janmashtami and the rich culinary heritage of Lucknow. The event provided a wonderful platform for students to showcase their creativity, culinary skills, and appreciation for traditional Indian cuisine.

The Three Young Culinary Stars

The highlight of the event was the participation of three talented students. Eva Mishra of Class 9E prepared the delicate and much-loved Makkhan Malai, while Satakshi of Class 9M presented Makhana Kheer. Jiya Chauhan of Class 9M prepared the royal Shahi Tukda.

Each dish reflected not only the students' culinary talent but also the richness and diversity of traditional cuisine associated with our cultural heritage.

Appreciation and Encouragement

The students proudly presented their creations to Abha Anant Ma'am, Principal, and Savita Mall Ma'am, Vice Principal, who appreciated their efforts and enthusiasm.

The in-charges, Swarnlata Ma'am, Archana Ma'am, Diksha Ma'am, and Sadaf Ma'am, also encouraged and appreciated the students for their dedication and creativity. The showstoppers were truly a great sight for all of us to see and get inspired from!

Adding a special touch to the event were the beautifully designed badges made by Bhavya and Avishi, which made the young participants stand out and added to the festive atmosphere.

Janmashtami and Lucknow's Living Heritage

Janmashtami holds a special place in Lucknow's cultural landscape, reflecting the city's tradition of celebrating festivals with shared joy, art, music, and food. Known for its beautiful Ganga-Jamuni tehzeeb, Lucknow has long embraced festivals as occasions that bring communities together.

The celebrations also carry a strong culinary connection, with traditional festive dishes becoming a part of the occasion. Thus, Janmashtami in Lucknow is not just a religious celebration, but also a reflection of the city's living heritage, cultural harmony, and love for its traditions.

A Celebration to Remember

The three young chefs truly became the showstoppers of the day, turning traditional recipes into a celebration of skill, culture, and creativity. Through this event, Anjuman-e-Tareekh celebrated not only Janmashtami but also the living culinary heritage of Lucknow, reminding us that traditions continue to thrive when they are passed on to the next generation.
