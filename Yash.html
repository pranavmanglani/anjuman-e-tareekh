The Beginning ─

Where every meal
became a memory.

Awadh was never merely a place on a map. It was a meeting of courtly refinement, local abundance, and the unhurried art of welcome.

Centred around Lucknow and Faizabad, Awadhi culture grew through many hands, languages, traditions, and kitchens. Food became one of its most enduring forms of identity.

The Royal Kitchen ─

Patience was
the secret spice.

Behind every silver cloche was a choreography of heat, time, and fragrance. The degchi was sealed. The fire was lowered. And the kitchen waited.
